·       Update on documentary film project about PBB that will feature the CAG – Colin Gray (TBD)

·       The CAG General Membership Meetings on May 21st and June 18th will be in-person only.

·       CMU Communications interviewed Rebecca Terpening about her Public Health Program internship with the MMDHD, Michigan PBB Registry, and EPA EJ grant (Alma College): [https://www.cmich.edu/news/details/cmu-undergrad-provides-hope-through-internship](https://www.cmich.edu/news/details/cmu-undergrad-provides-hope-through-internship). ·       Funding for a US EPA Environmental Justice grant awarded to Alma College and focused on developing curriculum on the PBB Disaster and Velsicol Sites for secondary students and vocational training programs was revoked in late March. The project team planned to participate in county fairs, distribute curriculum to Michigan educators, and host a professional development workshop for teachers in partnership with the Archives of Michigan.

·       Doug Brecht continued to share news articles about changes implemented and proposed at the federal level that could impact the Velsicol Sites cleanup and Pine River.

·       The CAG sent comments and questions about remedial work in OU3 to US EPA on March 27, 2025. We eagerly await a response.

·       Members participated in an Earth Day program at Alma College on Saturday, April 5th. Gary, Jane, Margaret and Gary Hoyt, and Brittany displayed CAG materials in a pop-up exhibit, as well as shared materials related to our Site and community history with students, faculty, staff, and alumni.

·       In response to official communication from the CAG last month, the Executive Committee met with US Senator Elissa Slotkin’s Mid-Michigan Region Manager, Jeremy Blatt, on Wednesday, April 16th (tonight). We hope the Senator and her staff will tour the FPS and meet with community members, City officials, and agency partners this summer or fall.

·       The CAG is still waiting on a response to our FOIA for the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission records related to radioactive waste at the FPS (submitted in October 2024; still in progress).
