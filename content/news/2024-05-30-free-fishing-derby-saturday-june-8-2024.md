On Saturday, June 88, 2024, we will have the annual Joe Scholtz Memorial Free Fishing Derby on the Pine River in St. Louis, Michigan. You can REGISTER ONLINE ([https://www.stlouismi.com/residents/events/fishing-derby/](https://www.stlouismi.com/residents/events/fishing-derby/)) again this year if you’d like to enter the Joe Scholtz Memorial Free Fishing Derby in St. Louis, coming up Saturday, June 8!

The fishing derby is named for former mayor and Pine River Task Force member Joe Scholtz. While clean-up of the Pine River is continuing, the derby is a time for celebrate the recreational value and need for a cleaned and protected river.

Please pass this along to anyone you think might be interested – always a fun, family event.  😊

Here’s the link to register on:  (please do this as soon as you can so that event organizers can get everything ready ahead of time)

[https://www.stlouismi.com/residents/events/fishing-derby/](https://www.stlouismi.com/residents/events/fishing-derby/)

[Joe Scholtz Mem. Fishing Derby - St Louis MI](https://www.stlouismi.com/residents/events/fishing-derby/)

Participants in the Joe Scholtz Memorial Free Fishing Derby must preregister for the event. This annual event is scheduled during Michigan's Free Fishing Weekend, so no fishing license is required to participate. All ages are welcome.
