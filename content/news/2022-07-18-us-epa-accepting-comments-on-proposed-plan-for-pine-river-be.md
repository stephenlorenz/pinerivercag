EPA is accepting comments on the proposed clean-up for “Operable Unit 3,” the EPA term for the Pine River for the first 1.5 miles below the St. Louis Dam. EPA will be conducting a meeting on the proposed plan on July 26, 2022 virtually from 6:00 p.m. through 7:30 p.m. To attend the meeting on-line, on July 26 go to [http://tinurl.com/Velsicol-Meeting-Link.](http://tinurl.com/Velsicol-Meeting-Link.) You also can join the meeting by phone 872-813-0592. the phone conference ID is: 109 773 416#

You also can join the meeting by going to www.epa.gov/superfund/velsicol-chemical-michigan
