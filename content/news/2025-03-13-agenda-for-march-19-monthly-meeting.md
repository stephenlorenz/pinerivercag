**4. Correspondence and Communication** - Brittany Fremion, Chairperson

· Update on documentary film project about PBB that will feature the CAG – Colin Gray (TBD)

· The Morning Sun published an article on Margaret Hoyt’s induction to the CAG Hall of Fame (appended).

· CAG officers and members participated in the online Michigan PBB Registry community meeting at the beginning of March. A recording of the meeting will be posted to the Registry website (Emory University).

· Doug Brecht shared news articles about changes implemented and proposed at the federal level that could impact the Velsicol Sites cleanup and Pine River.

· The CAG Executive Committee met remotely the evening of Thursday, March 13th, to discuss downstream work, a possible TA candidate, a gift membership program proposal and donation, and collaboration with Alma community organizations for an Earth Day event.

· The CAG is still waiting on a response to our FOIA for the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission records related to radioactive waste at the FPS (submitted in October 2024).

· The CAG wrote an official letter to RFK, Jr., new Secretary of US DHHS, to remind him of the Michigan PBB Disaster and prior visit to St. Louis and the Velsicol Sites in the early 2000s.

· The CAG also wrote and sent an official letter to US Senators Gary Peters and Elissa Slotkin and Congressman John Moolenaar inviting them to participate in CAG meetings, as well as underscoring the difference the federal/US EPA cleanup of the Velsicol Chemical Corporation Superfund Sites has made to the City of St. Louis and residents of the Pine River watershed.

· Several CAG members participated in EPA community interviews in early March, providing feedback on communication and engagement with the Region 5 team.

· Gary and Brittany will prepare and submit the first TAG quarterly report to EPA at the end of the month.
