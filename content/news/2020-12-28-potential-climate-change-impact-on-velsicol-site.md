In a widely reported story today, a partnership between [Inside Climate News](https://insideclimatenews.org) a nonprofit, independent news outlet that covers climate, energy and the environment, and [The Texas Observer](https://www.texasobserver.org) a nonprofit investigative news outlet reported on the combined threat of climate change and Superfund sites.&nbsp; The Velsicol site in St. Louis was one of the sites singled out for concern, especially because of flooding possibilities.&nbsp; For more on this go to: [Biden will inherit hundreds of toxic waste Superfund sites, with climate threats looming (nbcnews.com)](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/biden-will-inherit-hundreds-toxic-waste-superfund-sites-climate-threats-n1252276).
