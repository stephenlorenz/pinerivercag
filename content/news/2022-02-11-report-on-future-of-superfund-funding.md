U.S. PIRG has released a new report on funding for the Superfund program following the enactment of the Infrastructure Act passed by Congress in fall 2021. The Infrastructure Act has brought much more money and clean-ups to sites across the U.S., including in St. Louis, Michigan. See the US PIRG report here: [CTP_CTE_FundingSuperfund.pdf (connpirg.org)](https://connpirg.org/sites/pirg/files/reports/CTP_CTE_FundingSuperfund.pdf)
