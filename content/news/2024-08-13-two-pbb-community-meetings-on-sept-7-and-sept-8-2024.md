We want the general public to know they are invited to participate in a community information meeting in early September. The PBB Leadership Team will be holding the meetings on **Sept. 7, 2024 in Mt. Pleasant** and on **Sept. 8 in Big Rapids.** The meetings are free and open to the public. We will share **new research results** **and resources for you to share with your healthcare providers.**

Below are details:

**Saturday, September 7th meeting**

Time: 10:00 AM - 12:00PM

Location: **Central Michigan University, College of Medicine (1280 East Campus Drive, Room 1404, Mount Pleasant, MI 48859)**

**NOTE: Parking is free at the College of Medicine.**

**Sunday, September 8th meeting**

Time: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Location: **Hampton by Hilton Big Rapids (15320 Waldron Way, Big Rapids, MI 49307)**

We will not be mailing postcard invitations, so please share the meeting information with friends and family, especially those who do not use email. Thank you!

We hope to see you in September,

The Emory PBB Research Team

[https://pbbregistry.emory.edu](https://pbbregistry.emory.edu/)

[https://www.facebook.com/PBBRegistry](https://www.facebook.com/PBBRegistry)
