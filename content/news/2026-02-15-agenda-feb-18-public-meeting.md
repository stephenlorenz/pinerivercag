·        We seek help distributing the *Science Journal for Kids* article and companion curriculum for middle and high school students when it’s published. The article translates a [PBB State of the Science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40293296/) paper on health impacts from PBB exposure in both English and Spanish. The CAG funded the project (not with federal dollars).
