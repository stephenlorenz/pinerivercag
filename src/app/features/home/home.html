Est. 1998 · Pine River Watershed, Michigan

Restoring the Pine River to its pristine natural beauty — and the health of the community that calls it home.

The Pine River Superfund Citizen Task Force is a volunteer-run nonprofit that advises the EPA on cleaning up decades of chemical contamination in Michigan's Pine River watershed — and makes sure the community stays in the room while it happens.

Advise

We sit at the table with the EPA

Task force members review remediation plans and represent community concerns directly to the agencies responsible for cleanup.

Monitor

We track what's actually happening

From meeting minutes to water sampling reports, we keep a public record of the remediation as it unfolds.

Inform

We translate it for everyone else

Superfund science is dense. Our glossary, timeline, and lessons pages exist to make it legible to neighbors, not just regulators.
